# 35: The coronary bed leaves the map

Type: task
Status: open
Blocked by: 09

**Why.** [Record 0002](../../../docs/adr/0002-map-starts-grouped.md) keeps a split only when a sourced check fails without it. [Ticket 01](01-rung-0-specification.md) added the coronary bed as a model default and named the run that would earn it: the slice's posture checks with arrow 90 wired, if paying the heart's work at the muscle bed let the heart's demand open the leg's vessels as the body stands. [Ticket 09](09-tissues-respire.md) ran it. The heart's work falls on standing, no bed owes oxygen at any second, and the checks give the same verdict with the bed and without it. So the bed is not earned.

Removing it re-groups the body. In two prototype groupings the resting output fell by 4.2% and by 6.5%, and where the bed's conductances went with it the muscle bed's cells came within 1.8 mmHg of owing. So the removal gets a plan of its own.

- [ ] Plan: a grouping in which the muscle bed takes the heart's oxygen demand and its flow, with the body's resting output, arterial pressure and total oxygen use where they were, each bed's margin above the pressure at which it owes, and the posture checks' verdicts beside the body's with the bed
- [ ] The heart's tissue has no bed of its own: its cells' holds, the map's lists and the tables' rows go, and the muscle bed pays the heart's work
- [ ] The tests that stage a law at the coronary bed move to another bed
- [ ] Every bound measured again, each pin that moves with its sentence, and the cost line before and after
- [ ] Ticket 01's rows for the coronary bed, and the tickets that name it, say what the map holds

**Non-goals:** coronary territories and perfusion by the phase of the beat, which SRS section 2 names for later rungs. A clinician's question that needs them brings a sourced target and earns the split (record 0002).
