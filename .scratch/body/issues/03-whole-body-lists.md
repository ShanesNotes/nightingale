## Answer

The contract's closed lists grew once, in two PRs: nightingale#124 for the species and the charge rule, and nightingale#126 for places and routes. pstack `architect` ran an Opus and a Codex design at max effort with a Codex judge at max; the judge picked the Opus design, 24 to 21, and the split (`~/lanes/logs/ng-body-wave-2/03-whole-body-lists/architect/`). Every value is synthetic or chemistry: no clinical number entered.

- **Species.** One `species!` table (`crates/ng-contract/src/species.rs`) states each of 40 species once, and the crate derives its marker type, makeup, names, dimension, whether water carries it, the kinds of space that may hold it, its charge after speciation and whether it counts in osmolality. The twelve existing rows keep their order, strings and discriminants. The 28 new ones are nitrogen gas, glycogen, triglyceride, fatty acids, glycerol, amino acids, body protein, lactate, ketones, urea, ammonium, creatinine, the fixed acid, the diet base, the cell anion, calcium, magnesium, phosphate, hemoglobin, iron, insulin, glucagon, renin, angiotensin II, aldosterone, the natriuretic peptide, parathyroid hormone and cortisol. Nitrogen joins the elements.
- **Charge.** `Makeup::Carried { charge }` is the only makeup with a fixed charge, and `Reactant` exists only for a species that states atoms, so no reaction names a charged species. The compile's charge leg could no longer be reached by any code or test, so it retired, and so did `NoMakeup`. Each acid and base states its uncharged atoms, but for the fixed acid (below); the table's charge-after-speciation column feeds the charge membership rows, which ticket 18's balance reads.
- **Places.** `Space::Gas` holds no water. Every capillary bed and every tissue site has one, and a schema pools gas there where it sits: a lung bed's alveolar gas and the conducting airway's. A capillary bed may state its `Tissue`, so `Beds<T>` and `Besides<T>` name one tissue's beds or every other; `Alimentary` and `Airway` name a network's sites. The networks gain the airway and the gut, with `AirwayEdge`, `GutEdge` and `AbsorbingEdge`. Nine landmarks join the slice's four. Each new hormone enters and leaves at sites body 01 names (lines 602 to 613): the portal vein, the glomerular and pulmonary beds' plasma, the liver bed and the central veins, all on the map already, and each leaves by a first-order clearance as vasopressin does; the landmarks say where a gland reads, and ticket 04's ports and boundary routes carry each hormone in and out. Compile refuses a pool in a space its species may not hold (`OutOfPlace`), by kind of space.
- **Routes.** `Across(Wall, Sites, Species)` crosses a bed's walls, `Along(Passage, Species)` runs along a network, and `Heat(HeatRoute)` moves energy between each bed's heat, the core store and the other stores, with the pumps' work from the receipt `chemical energy`. The core store is the containment root's. Four ports in the slice's scopes name entries; ticket 04 adds the rest. One `Membrane` gives a wall's conductance and reflection for a species; on a fluid route its reflection lowers as `reflect` does, and on a wall's or a passage's own route the law reads it.
- **Identities.** `Osmoles` and the charge and osmole membership rows; two cancellation rows (a coefficient in U per V times a V is a U; a U over a U per V is a V), which hold Henry's law, conduction, blood's heat, a partial pressure and a store's temperature from its heat; and ratio rows for length, temperature, flow, charge and osmoles. A product over one of its factors has no row, because it made a concentration's species ambiguous. Both cancellation rows take only `Plain` dimensions, which hold no set point and no deviation at any depth, so neither leaves its own dimension through them. Through them a species reaches a charge or osmole sum only by a declared parameter in charge or osmoles per that species, or by another expression already in charge or osmoles, which ticket 18's weak acids need; the table's relabels stay the only direct way in.
- **Held by tests.** Compile-fail probes, each with its code and legal twin: a fixed charge on atoms (E0559), a carried reactant (E0277), a species outside a charge or osmole sum at the amount and the concentration level (E0277), a tissue on a site that is no bed (E0308), a coefficient against another numerator or denominator (E0277, one for each row), a set point or deviation carried out through a cancellation row at any depth (E0277, four probes), and a dimensioned reflection (E0308). A test pins each new row's makeup, carriage and spaces as the table states them, departures included, and another pins which of all 40 rows count in osmolality. Refusals and pins with mutant rows their tests catch: `species-in-its-spaces`, `cell-anion-stays-in-cells`, `glycerol-stays-out-of-osmolality`, `tissue-served-once`, `airway-fit`, `airway-fit-into`, `gut-fit`, `gut-fit-out-of`, `besides-leaves-out-its-tissue`, and the core store's row. The slice's model hash, conserved basis and both first-second traces in `tests/trace.rs` did not move.
