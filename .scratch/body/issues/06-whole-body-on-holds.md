# 06: The whole body compiles on holds

Type: task
Status: claimed
Claimed by: body-06-p3, then body-06-p4a for part 4a
Blocked by: 04, 05

**What to build:** A second assembly beside the slice: the whole body. It has every site, space, pool and route the 13 systems need at rung 0, and every law the slice lacks is a declared stand-in owned by its card, as the slice already holds the nine cards outside it. The slice stays as it is, so the arena's fixture does not move while cores are scored on it, and it stays the reference a deeper body is compared with.

- [x] The body compiles, admits at 06:55 on its synthetic fixture and passes the certificate on a short window with its held support reported (`body_run.rs`)
- [x] A table of every hold by card: what it fixes and which ticket releases it. This table is the build's to-do list, and the count of holds left is its progress. It takes [ticket 01](01-rung-0-specification.md)'s rows. The airway's exit for water and heat is a held route of the respiratory card beside the slice's skin and heat exits, and the liver bed's edges are held under the hepatic card until ticket 14 (`body.rs`, `each_hold_names_the_ticket_that_releases_it_and_its_body_01_line`)
- [x] A hold names one species in a space at the sites of one site set (`HeldDeclaration::pool`), so tickets 08, 09, 14 to 19 and 21 can each release their species in the cells, ticket 15 at the muscle and fat beds alone
- [x] Each fluid route states, for each new species, whether it carries it or keeps it back, since the compiler refuses a stranded cargo. Keeping back is the stand-in wherever a later ticket owns the crossing. A keep-back is stated for a route family, not for a site, so a species held at some beds' walls and carried at others has none: iron is that case, and the body settles it by carrying iron in blood and holding its interstitium at the liver and gut beds (`keep_back.rs`)
- [x] A test pins the hold table, as the slice's tests pin its nine holds, so a PR that adds or releases a hold shows it (`body.rs`, `the_hold_table_is_the_compiled_bodys_coverage_manifest_row_for_row`)
- [x] Iron's pools in cells stand at the liver and gut beds and at no other bed, asserted on the whole body's rows. Ticket 04 grants HC's iron crossing of the cell membrane at every capillary bed by type, and only the schema's placement of iron's pools holds it to body 01's two beds. HC's iron in cells stays held in this body until [ticket 21](21-blood-cells-and-systems-at-rest.md) (01:884), and compile refuses a law that moves a held pool (`HeldPoolMoved`), so the crossing has no column here. Ticket 21 asserts the crossing's columns. Iron's blood pools cannot be held: GI's meal route delivers iron into the portal vein, so circulation carries iron from this ticket on (01:334), and 01:306's ticket 21 holds for the wall and the cells only (`body.rs`, `iron_lives_in_cells_at_the_liver_and_gut_beds_only`)
- [ ] The replay gates pass on the body. Each gate runs a core several times over its run, so the gates' time grows with the body's cost, and [ticket 26](26-cost-budget.md) caps it
- [x] The body has its own expect trace and its own size pins, so a system ticket updates the body's and never the slice's (`body_run.rs` and `body.rs`, `the_whole_body_holds_its_rows_columns_and_digests`)
- [x] Cost: unknowns, rows and milliseconds a step for the body on holds against the slice, measured on one named build worker. [Ticket 26](26-cost-budget.md) sets the budgets from this (part 4, below)
- [x] The body's packet sets four held exits to zero until the laws that fill their stores land, because a constant exit from a store nothing fills empties it. RS's airway heat leaves the airway's heat store, which MT's conduction fills at [ticket 12](12-heat-balance.md). MT's dry heat loss and skin evaporative heat leave the skin heat store, which ticket 12 fills (01:168-170). RS's expired water leaves the airway gas, which [ticket 10](10-lungs-breathe.md) fills from the pulmonary bed's interstitium (01:63) (`body_run.rs`: none of the four routes moves in the certificate's held support)
- [ ] Owed: an inert keep-back line is not tied to its law module. `KEEP_BACKS` mirrors each route's `keeps_back` lines, and an inert line keeps back a species that neither end of its route holds, so deleting Starling's `keeps_back::<Ammonium>` passes every test. Tying the two needs an accessor in the law crates that lists each route's keep-back lines

**Non-goals:** any new law. A stand-in is a constant or the simplest balanced flow that lets the body compile.

From [ticket 04](04-scopes-ports-and-census.md):

- The liver bed's edges are their own family, `Sinusoids`, which the hepatic card owns, and `Circulation` reaches every other vascular edge. This body holds HP through `hold`, lends HP's builder to the stand-ins with `HeldScope::stand_ins()`, and then hands it on with `hepatic(Some(hp))`. The stand-in for the liver bed's edges is written through `HepaticPorts::sinusoids` on that builder. An HP held whole through `HeldScope::parts()` has no such port, so compile refuses the liver bed's edges as a law omitted under HP (`OmittedLaw`, in `crates/ng-contract/tests/sinusoids.rs`).
- The renal card's reaction grant names the peritubular bed as `Beds<Tubule>`. So this body's map states `serves(peritubular bed, Tubule)`. The slice's map never states it, because that would write a tissue into the slice's anatomy digest.
- The pleural fluid cannot be left out while GI's abdominal enclosure is held. `leave_out` leaves out a kind of space at every site, and a held enclosure's fluid must exist (`crates/ng-contract/tests/held.rs:717`). Body 01 makes the pleural fluid a left-out space (01:362, 491, 713), so in this body both enclosures' fluids are held pools. A built RS that places the pleural fluid with no law to move it has an orphan pool (`crates/ng-contract/tests/scopes.rs:208`). Model default: once RS is built, it keeps an empty `HeldDeclaration` whose validity names the pleural fluid, so that fluid stays held (`crates/ng-contract/tests/scopes.rs:297`). The alternative is a leave-out for one site, which changes the contract. [Ticket 10](10-lungs-breathe.md) meets the same rule.
- MT's conduction feeds the conducting airway's heat store, and [ticket 10](10-lungs-breathe.md)'s airway heat exit leaves from that store. This body builds no conduction stand-in. MT's held declaration (`holds/mt.rs`) holds its gas and fuel pools and serves its six skin exits, each at a constant rate, so nothing fills the airway's heat store or the skin heat store until [ticket 12](12-heat-balance.md) builds conduction. The packet's item in the list above sets the exits from those stores to zero until then.
- The slice and this body share the five system crates. A loop ticket that edits a law the slice runs therefore moves the slice. A keep-back of a species the slice does not place adds no column to the slice, so each route's keep-backs sit in that route's own law module (Starling and lymph in `ng-fluids`, filtration in `ng-renal`) and the slice does not move. A slice law that a later ticket must change in another way forks for this body, so the slice keeps its law.
- `Schema::process` places a boundary process at any site and space, and compile refuses only a pool the body lacks, so nothing checks where a process stands. This body places 49 of the 50 processes where body 01 puts it, such as insulin's secretion into the portal vein (01:605) and hemoglobin's production in the central veins (01:301), and its test pins each process's site and space. RN's `urine` process waits for [ticket 22](22-the-bladder.md): the slice's boundary drain has the same name, `Schema::process` refuses a name twice, and body 01 keeps the slice's drain until ticket 22 (01:60, 714, 885). Body 01 gives six processes no site or no space, so the body takes model defaults: nitrogen gas enters and leaves at the conducting airway's gas, as its other gases do; insulin's first pass leaves the liver bed's plasma; and skin evaporation and the three sweat exits leave the skin bed's interstitium, as the slice's skin water does.
- FL's generic wall ports can take a crossing that body 01 gives another card, and `CrossedTwice` refuses only a double. So this body's test pins FL's crossings by wall and species against 01:38.

From part 4 (the body admits, runs and is pinned):

- Main's body did not admit. GI's gut transit was a zero rate that read no state, so the ring could not place each ingested species between the stomach and the small intestine: the total fixed their sum and nothing fixed the split. The transit stand-in is now a balanced exchange, `gi.transit-exchange` times the stomach's amount less the small intestine's. It is zero at an equal start, so nothing moves. The value sets only how fast an unequal split would even out: any positive value admits to the same split. A second admission at 0.5 against the packet's 1e-3 admitted to the same 1,236 amounts, bit for bit. A body that leaves a split undetermined is refused at admission, never pinned to a guess. [Ticket 13](13-gut-absorbs-a-meal.md)'s gut motility replaces this stand-in.
- The packet carries the slice's values wherever the body keeps the slice's site, edge or binding. It adds two synthetic rules. Each of the five new parallel beds (brain, coronary, fat, skin and bone) is the leg bed at a tenth: its volumes, filtration and lymph conductance times 0.1, its elastances and resistances times 10. Every held card's stand-in constant is zero, so no pool moves until its card's ticket. That includes the four exits above. The liver bed takes the gut bed's values, because it carries the portal flow in series.

- Cost, from the bench's `arena` mode with `body` (`tools/bench`), on ryzen, three runs each, each its own job: the slice has 241 rows, 257 unknowns and 1,578 Jacobian nonzeros, admits in 14.9, 15.9 and 16.4 s and takes 0.545 to 0.575 ms a step over its first minute.
