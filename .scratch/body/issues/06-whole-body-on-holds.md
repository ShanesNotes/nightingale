From part 4c (the replay gates on the body):

- Admission's solve reads no core, and the admission system's identity holds the model, packet, anchor and cycle but not the port. So `solve_admission` solves once on a port with no input, and `admit_from` admits each core and either port from that solve, checked as `admit_at` checks its own. One case type, `AssemblyCase`, serves the slice (`SliceCase`, same name and behaviour) and the body (`BodyCase`): one solve, then one start for each core resolution, with the inputs and without. A body case admits once instead of four times.
- A start from the shared solve refuses an input before the fence, the first instant admission does not read, because its solve read a port with none. The fence is admission's own: `InitializationSystem::fence` reads it, `AdmissionSolve` keeps it, `admit_from` takes the events and refuses any stamped before it with `EventBeforeFence` naming it, and the case places its stand and bolus at it. On the slice and the body it is the anchor plus one second, the packets' cycle, and on a two-second cycle it is the anchor plus two (`assembly_fence.rs`). No case in the repo's tests or fixtures is refused: the slice's and the body's inputs sit at the fence, and the other replay cases (`TwoReservoirs`, `ChanceCase` and the test wrappers) do not take the shared solve. Finding: on main, `admit_at`'s result depends on the port inside the cycle while the identity leaves the port out. On the slice with `Core::new(10 ms)`, the empty port admits, a stand and a 400 bolus at the anchor are refused at row 1 (`Inconsistent`), and the shared empty-port solve with that port is refused at the recheck, at row 8. So the refusal is the guard, and the recheck caught this one input only by its tolerance. Any `CaseAssembly`, the public way to the shared solve, reaches it through `admit_from`, so no caller reaches the shared solve without the refusal. [Native-contract 56](../../native-contract/issues/56-the-admission-identity-leaves-the-drive-frames-out.md) asks whether the admission identity should hold the frames, so the contract refuses such a workspace for every caller.
