# 38: The drive moves the breath's depth as well as its rate

Type: task
Status: open
Blocked by: 11, ../../research/issues/23-how-the-drive-splits-into-rate-and-depth.md, ../../native-contract/issues/87-a-firing-sets-a-memory-to-a-kernels-value.md

**Why.** The developer reviewer ruled the standard: the body's breathing control moves the breath's depth and its rate together, and rate alone is a step. [Ticket 11](11-breathing-is-controlled.md) closes the gas loop on the breath's rate. Ruled at planning, a model default open to the developer reviewer: depth lands before [ticket 28](28-two-synthetic-days.md)'s 48-hour run, through [ticket 24](24-the-synthetic-day.md), which waits on this ticket.

**What to build:** The nervous card's drive scale moves the breath's depth as well as its rate, in the split [research 23](../../research/issues/23-how-the-drive-splits-into-rate-and-depth.md) cites, on the whole body. Ticket 11's parts stay as they are: 11b is the rate and 11c is sleep. [Native-contract 87](../../native-contract/issues/87-a-firing-sets-a-memory-to-a-kernels-value.md)'s form, a firing at each breath edge that sets the breath's depth from the drive scale, is the first candidate for the build. Ticket 11's plan saw the drive scale on depth move the admitted rest, and both valve closings with it: if the build moves the rest, [native-contract 86](../../native-contract/issues/86-admission-reaches-a-moved-rest.md) is the fix.

The boxes, each with what shows it:

- [ ] Tests: depth and rate each follow the drive in the split research 23 cites, on a fixture, at stated points of the drive scale
- [ ] Run: on the body, the 10% rise in carbon dioxide production moves arterial carbon dioxide less than it does under rate alone. Both figures are in the PR, from the same admitted start and the same span
- [ ] Every link of the loop moves its reader the right way, on the body from the admitted start, as ticket 11 judges its links. Each link is judged over equal spans of whole breaths, since the breath's length and depth both move
- [ ] A body with both gains zero keeps the drive scale at its rest's bits. That body is the body at fixed ventilation, and its carbon dioxide moves to its own balance. It is the held run the link box judges against
- [ ] The certificate passes on the body under [native-contract 85](../../native-contract/issues/85-the-certificates-windows-end-on-breath-edges.md)'s windows between breath edges, and the body's trace is updated
- [ ] Cost: unknowns and rows before and after, and user instructions a simulated second against [ticket 26](26-cost-budget.md)'s budget, on the body's bench at the PR's base and its head. Ticket 11's plan measured depth on a continuous path at 238.2 M instructions a simulated second against rate's 41.2 M, as native-contract 87 quotes it, because the path from the gases to the pump's pressure joins the solver's largest dense block. Native-contract 87's form is the first candidate for paying less. If the prototype cannot bring depth inside the budget, the budget's owner records an exception with its reason in this ticket, under ticket 26's rule. Depth is not dropped
- [ ] One body run of an hour, outside the suite, as evidence in the PR, with each inventory kept at every breath edge as 11b's hour run keeps it. The PR says what the run shows: where carbon dioxide settles, and whether the drive scale swings
- [ ] The pins that move, and why for each: the model hash, the packet, the admitted seal, the first second and the first minute, as each one moves

**Non-goals:** sleep, which is 11c; the gains' sourced values, which are [research 22](../../research/issues/22-the-chemoreflex-gains-awake-and-asleep.md)'s; and periodic breathing.

Every value is synthetic. The split between depth and rate, and what bounds depth as the drive rises, are research 23's. The gains, and the steady change in arterial carbon dioxide the whole loop is judged against, are research 22's.
