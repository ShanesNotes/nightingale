**What to build:** Two heat stores at rung 0, the core store and the skin store, with blood carrying heat between them. Skin loses heat to the room by radiation, convection and evaporation. Evaporation is one rate that posts water and its latent heat through two routes in a fixed proportion, which the first box's test and a mutant row hold (the ruling on evaporation below). The thermostat reads core and skin temperature, the skin's through a skin thermoreceptor. It acts on the tone of skin vessels and on sweat through autonomic release at the skin junction, and on shivering through a somatic motor drive, arrow 87's shiver drive, which the muscle bed's heat reads. Sweat carries sodium and chloride with its water. Its set point follows the clock and steps down in sleep. Heat production comes from [ticket 09](09-tissues-respire.md) and changes with the day's activity. The body starts at its own heat balance, because a start at a textbook temperature drifts for hours.

**Releases:** the musculoskeletal card's hold on skin water loss, the slice's single heat exit, and coupling arrows 87 (its shiver drive; its set point and sweat drive stay held) and 91 (core temperature to the nervous system), and the respiratory card's hold on airway heat (`rs.airway-heat`). Arrows 92 to 94 carry temperature to blood, liver and blood chemistry, whose laws stay at a reference temperature at rung 0.

## Rulings

Each ruling is a model default, with its reason and what would change it.

1. **The plan is a measurement, and the build's order is ruled here.** Reason: the plan's split rested on two design questions it left open, and on a warm pass that showed no order.
2. **The thermal zero is 0 degrees,** 273.15 on the contract's absolute scale. A heat store's amount is energy above one declared thermal zero, and each store's reference is its capacity times its reference temperature above that zero. Reason: the first plan's reference left the core 0.00488 of a degree of cooling before its amount reached zero. It lands in 12a, the PR whose laws make the reference the store's level, never ahead of them, because on main alone it would be the worse start guess (inferred, no run). "Thermal zero" is in the glossary. What would change it: a reviewed packet that wants the amount to be a physical enthalpy.
3. **The second box is shown in three parts** and keeps its words "at admission". (a) is the PR's own test, not admission's recheck, which tests no tissue heat store's net over the cycle: it sums each tissue heat store's transfers over admission's own cycle and compares each store's net by itself with a tolerance in energy over the cycle. The tolerance is stated against heat made over the cycle and is no looser than main's today, 0.131441 J for the body's store and 0.002 J each for the skin heat site and the conducting airway, 0.135441 J together, so the thermal zero, which raises the recheck's tolerances to 17.2325 J together on the prototype, does not loosen it. If a run cannot meet it, the PR states the measure and stops for a ruling, and it does not relax the tolerance. (b) is recorded, not judged here, and [native-contract 100](../../native-contract/issues/100-the-body-moves-after-admission-what-moves-and-why.md)'s third box owns it. (c) is a mean over whole breaths, as a share of heat made, judged in every eligible window of a suite run, with its tolerance sized from that run's worst eligible window by native-contract 85's rule, quoted in the box. Reason: the body lives breathing, the instant is the heartbeat's phase, and the first beat with the breath held loses about 159 J on both bodies, far beyond either tolerance, for a cause not established. What would change it: native-contract 100 finding that cause, or a law that smooths the wall's dissipation inside the beat.
4. **Evaporation is one rate that posts water and its latent heat through two routes.** Only the evaporated water and its latent heat stand in a fixed proportion, which the first box's test and a mutant row hold, and no compound interface is built. In 12a the rate is the skin's diffusion term at rest, `w_d E_max`, with the forcing on it, where `E_max = LR h_c (P_sat(T_s) - phi P_sat(T_a)) F_pcl A` and `F_pcl = 1 / (1 + 0.143 h_c I_clo)`, so the room's air speed, through `h_c`, and clothing enter the evaporative capacity (research 12 lines 33 and 34). Water leaves the skin bed at the rate over `lambda`, and heat leaves the skin store at the rate. The sweat rate, the unevaporated sweat and the salts are 12b's. The sweat rate is its own rate (research 12 line 38). The unevaporated sweat is what is secreted beyond what evaporates, `(E_rsw - smooth_min(E_rsw, E_max, w)) / lambda` (research 12 line 34). The salts are charged on secreted sweat (research 12 line 38). Reason: the first box already holds the proportion by a test, and this is the smallest lasting form. What would change it: a second process that must post two species where a test cannot hold the proportion.
5. **The prototype's 0.404 sodium a second is not carried.** It is charged on evaporation, so it is not a resting figure. The salt balance above shows the body readmits with the kidney excreting less: that is what a salt route does to admission, and the PR that posts the salts, 12b, says what the renal fit does. The plan's flag on the sweat's sodium closes on that balance. What would change it: a resting sweat rate under research 12's sweat law that moves the renal fit beyond what its PR can state.
6. **Bindings.** No packet value is the admitted heat production or a multiple of it (SRS invariant I14). The prototype scales its capacities and several gains by one synthetic number copied from heat made, and the build has no such number. Each constant binds to its row in research 16 or carries a model-default label, and a PR that adds a packet key adds its row or its label. A packet derivation may use packet design values, never an admitted or produced one. The skin reference is a sourced parameter (NS-046), and the core's set point is a memory that follows the sourced target, so admission solves the temperatures and the admitted core temperature is a prediction that the healthy-range check judges. Research 16 binds NS-047 the target's mean from the thresholds, which needs one threshold fixed by convention, since only thresholds are measurable (research 16 line 971), and the PR that binds it states the convention, NS-048 to NS-051 the rhythm's harmonics, NS-052 sleep's step and NS-054 the sympathetic gain, with NS-053 the set point's speed and NS-055 and NS-056 the widths model defaults. MT-013 to MT-015 bind the specific heat and the skin's and core's masses only, MT-028 the latent heat, MT-035 the sweat gland's maximal rate and MT-036 the local skin temperature's effect. The glossary's line that admission solves each set point to its sensor's mean over the beat is about the respiratory chemoreceptors and is not this ticket's convention. Reason: I14. What would change it: a reviewed packet.
7. **The third box.** The cool pass shows the order of the commands and shivering's heat on the whole body. It does not show a vessel narrowing, and the warm pass shows no order, because in the prototype no command reaches a vessel. The box's vessel part is discharged only once the skin arteriole's resistance reads the command. Reason: the skin bed's flow above. What would change it: nothing short of that law.
