# 08: Blood carries oxygen and carbon dioxide

Type: task
Status: open
Blocked by: 06, `.scratch/research/issues/11-law-forms-gas-loop.md`

**What to build:** Oxygen and carbon dioxide are amounts in every blood and tissue space, moved by the flows that already move water, and crossing the capillary wall and the cell membrane by diffusion. Blood chemistry turns each total into its partial pressure and its saturation as algebraic rows that never make or lose mass. Hemoglobin is a mass in blood, so its concentration follows dilution, and the hematocrit it gives sets viscosity, which the vessels take as a constant today. The lungs and the tissues stay held: a fixed exchange at the lung and a fixed use at each bed.

**Releases:** from ticket 06's hold table, gas content in blood and tissue, the carrier's capacity, and viscosity on coupling arrow 114.

- [ ] Tests: content against partial pressure matches each form at stated points, and each gas's total is conserved through speciation to the ledger's floor
- [ ] Run: with the held exchange and use, arterial and venous contents settle where the Fick identity puts them. A water bolus lowers hemoglobin concentration and arterial content while the oxygen amount holds
- [ ] The certificate passes on the whole body, and the body's trace is updated with one sentence on why it moved
- [ ] Cost: unknowns and rows before and after, and wall time for each simulated second against [ticket 07](07-cost-budget-and-step-plan.md)'s budget

**Non-goals:** acid-base beyond what carriage needs (ticket 18), abnormal hemoglobins, and red cell turnover (ticket 21).
