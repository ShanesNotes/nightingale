## The four PRs

The ticket lands as four PRs: 10a the alveolar unit and its exchange, 10b the breath and the pleural pressure (arrow 82), 10c the pump and its work (arrows 95, 84 and 85), 10d the lung's water. The plan and its prototypes are outside the repo. Tidal breathing is the plan's pick: on the body it costs 5.5 M a simulated second over quasi-steady alveolar gas at the same counts, and only tidal gas swings the alveolar and arterial gases within a breath.

- 10a gives the respiratory card its own crate, `ng-respiratory`. Its laws hold oxygen, carbon dioxide and nitrogen gas in the dead space (the conducting airway's gas) and the alveolar unit (the pulmonary bed's gas). Each gas's volume is its amount times a molar volume, and its share is that volume over the dry gas's.
- Along the airway edge and across the room's side of the dead space the gas moves as one volume flow, `G (h+ f_up - h- f_down) + M (f_up - f_down)` for each gas's volume share `f`, and each gas's amount moves at that over its molar volume: bulk flow at one airway conductance `G` (volume for each unit of pressure) that carries the upwind end's share, and a mixing exchange `M` (volume a second) that stands for the breath's mean ventilation until 10b builds the breath. The room's side carries the inspired share `F_I` inward, with nitrogen gas's share the rest of oxygen's and carbon dioxide's, as the glossary's Room conditions has it, so the shares sum to one by the law. At rest the smooth parts of the heads add 78 a second each way beside the mixing exchange's 3,240, since the resting head sits inside the head's width. Since each end's shares sum to one, the mixing exchange moves no net volume whatever the molar volumes are; the contract's identity table gains the one generic row this needs, a volume flow over a gas's molar volume is that gas's rate (`Quotient<Rate<Volume>, Quotient<Volume, Q>>`), with a compile-fail doctest that a non-substance is refused. Stated per gas, as the first build had it, three conductances and three mixing rates told one quantity three ways, and with unequal molar volumes the mixing moved net volume: an independent check's test put the alveolar unit at 4,979.0 where no net volume crosses, against 5,000. The alveolar unit's head is its elastic recoil, `-E_L (V - V_L0) - E_W (V - V_W0)`, read from the edge's downstream amounts, so no row carries the lung's volume. The dead space's head is its own, `E_D (V_D0 - V_D)`, at the room's side: with the alveolar unit's head there, as the plan's prototype had it, the dead space's total is conserved by its laws and nothing sets it.
- Alveolar exchange is Fick's law on the card's alveolar route, `J_i = D_i ((P_B - P_H2O) f_i - P_c,i)`, reading the pulmonary bed's plasma through the respiratory speciation reader, now wired. The membrane reads the blood side through a `Plasma` trait: speciation on the body, a dissolved stand-in on the one-unit fixture.
- The shunt is a vascular edge from the pulmonary artery to the pulmonary vein at `R_c (1 - f) / f`, `f` = 0.02 beside the bed's 0.09. The pulmonary bed's inflow reads no oxygen debt (`cv.debt-share` 0, as the peritubular bed's): the lung's vessels answer alveolar oxygen, which ticket 01 leaves to a later rung, not their tissue's debt. Read at the bed's share, a debt of 1e-3 cut the shunt's fraction to 1.67% (an independent check's test); with no debt read it keeps 2%.
- The exchange reads the pulmonary bed at its own site, the alveolar route's, so it needs no `only_at`. Metabolism's held lung exchange goes: its two boundary routes, `mt.alveolar-oxygen`, `mt.alveolar-carbon-dioxide`, the two exchange conductances, `mt.lung-share` and its two `sum_at` rows.
- The room's pressure and its inspired fractions stay parameters (`rs.room-pressure`, `rs.oxygen-inspired` and the rest). Every whole-body admission, `tools/bench` among them, states no first frame, so a law that read `room_pressure()` would be refused `InputUnstated` until each one does.
