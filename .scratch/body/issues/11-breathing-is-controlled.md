# 11: Breathing is controlled

Type: task
Status: open
Blocked by: 05, 10, `.scratch/research/issues/11-law-forms-gas-loop.md`

**What to build:** The nervous system sets the drive to breathe. A central sensor reads the acidity that blood chemistry states for a brain space from ticket 08, which follows carbon dioxide while the anions are held, and a peripheral sensor reads arterial oxygen and carbon dioxide. The drive reaches the pump as release at its junctions. Sleep shifts the set point and lowers the gain. This closes the gas loop with its own controller.

Research 11 gives no form for stretch, and the loop held in every run of the plan without it.

## The plan, ruled at planning

The plan and its prototypes are outside the repo. Every figure below is from it, measured on the beelink beside other jobs with the load named, unless it says inferred. Every value is synthetic.

- **Who owns what.** The nervous card owns the breath's rhythm (its modes, phase and start hazard), the pump drive, the mixing share, both set points and the drive scale, a factor on the breath's rate that is one at the admitted rest. They move from the musculoskeletal card as 10c moved them there. The musculoskeletal card keeps the pump's force law and the held fatigue.
- **The drive reaches the pump as release**, in the nervous card's existing form `q_i f` at every junction that holds acetylcholine. The release law weights two traffics by site shares, `q_i (s_v f_vagal + s_m a)`: the sinoatrial junction keeps the vagal traffic and the respiratory pump's junction takes the pump drive `a` (shares 1, 0 and 0, 1). The receptor card's binding and effect laws run there with fast site values (release 0.05, reuptake 50, on 10, off 50 a second, efficacy 1), so the effect follows the drive within about 40 ms. The pump's force is `P_mus = PImax E` for the receptor's effect `E` at the pump's junction, research 11's pump row.
- **The controller moves the breath's rate, not its depth.** On the body's first minute depth removed twice what rate did of the open loop's rise in alveolar carbon dioxide (28.884 and 28.931 against main's 28.978), and offline on the packet's breath forms alveolar ventilation's relative sensitivity to the drive scale is 1.296 for depth and 0.778 for rate. Ruled at planning for cost: depth adds 238.2 M instructions a simulated second and rate 41.2 M, and the pick with the junction reads 53.7 M (loads 5.2 to 6.7). Depth's drive scale feeds the pump's pressure, the pleural pressure and the circulation, so the controller's chain joins the solver's largest dense block (644 rows to 657); rate's feeds only the breath's phase, which only guards read. The gains under rate are raised by 1/0.778 over depth's to give the same loop.
- **The central sensor** reads the brain bed's plasma acidity at the central chemoreceptor landmark, through the nervous card's speciation row at the brain bed, turned from held to wired. It needs no memory, since the bed's own carbon dioxide stores lag it. Its beat ripple puts about 1% on the drive scale, which rate control turns into a breath length moved by under 1%.
- **The peripheral sensor** reads arterial saturation and carbon dioxide at the sampled systemic arteries, at the peripheral chemoreceptor landmark, as research 11's form `G_p (1 - S_a) smooth_max(PaCO2 - B_p, 0, w)` does. The peripheral speciation row wires oxygen saturation in place of oxygen partial pressure. Over the first minutes the peripheral arm carries the loop, mostly through saturation; the central arm moves over minutes.
- **The set points** are slow memories in the baroreflex's form, so admission solves each to its sensor's mean over the beat, and the drive scale is one at admission whatever the packet says. Ruled at planning: admission's rest mode does not change. The set points are the admitted body's gases, which the tidal breath under-ventilates, so the breathing body settles above them by the loop's residual (alveolar carbon dioxide +0.34 at 10 minutes at gain 1, still creeping, under depth).
- **The loop.** Ruled at planning: the build keeps a gain at which no swing shows until research binds it. Under depth at four times the gain, the drive scale swung once (0.981 at 12 s, 1.054 at 37 s) and settled by 55 s; at gain 1 it showed none. The open loop is far from steady at 10 minutes, since the tissue stores give a time constant of tens of minutes (inferred from the linear creep), so the loop's gain cannot be read from a body run under an hour. Rate's loop was run for one minute only.
- **Sleep**, run on the depth prototype: `C = 1 - W s + (1 - L s) ((x_p - r_p) / w_p - (pH - r_c) / w_c)`, with `s` the sleep input, `W` the wake drive (0.15) and `L` the gain's fall (0.4), research 11's sleep row written about the set points. From a warm admission, awake at the anchor and asleep at 60 s, the drive scale fell from 1.0196 to 0.862 in the first sleeping breath and recovered to 0.952 by 840 s, and arterial carbon dioxide rose from 30.01 to 31.20, still rising. Under rate the same scale lengthens breaths (inferred).
- **Every link moves its reader the right way.** On the suite's one admitted start, the breath's own under-ventilation is the perturbation, and the first minute's breath means are compared with main's pinned open-loop figures. Under the pick the drive scale reads breath length, 4.86 to 4.88 s at 50 to 60 s against main's 5.000. Alveolar carbon dioxide read 28.991 against the pin's 28.978, the wrong side, because the junction's lag lowers the tidal volume 2.1% and the prototype did not refit the pump; 11a's refit restores that margin. A wrong sign shows as the scale falling while carbon dioxide rises, breaths longer than main's, or alveolar carbon dioxide over the pin. The central arm moves the scale only 0.2% in a minute, so the fixture stages its sign.
- **The certificate.** Every breathing bound 10b set is the most a breath moves an inventory, so it holds at any rate, and rate control moves the rate by a few percent awake (inferred from 10b's rule; the build's certificate test shows it). No bound tightens before native-contract 85. Once 85's windows end on breath edges, steady figures over whole breaths set the bounds, and the loop halves the steady carbon dioxide creep, so carbon and oxygen tighten.

- **The residual.** The set points are the admitted body's gases, so the breathing body settles above them by the loop's residual. The reviewed packet's fit closes it: a fit of the pump to the tidal body's own ventilation, with no contract change.
- **The gain on the body**, which needs runs of an hour. The hour-long runs of 11b and 11c are evidence, not a binding.
