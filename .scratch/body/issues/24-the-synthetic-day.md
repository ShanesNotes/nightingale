**Non-goals:** a 48 h run and pass 2, which are [ticket 28](28-two-synthetic-days.md), and any clinical claim.

From [ticket 08](08-blood-carries-gases.md): the body's gas drift bounds, oxygen's and carbon dioxide's, are fixture data for its undisturbed start, twice each gas's worst ten-second change over the first minute. A disturbed run goes far past them: after a 400 mL water bolus the check of the second PR read oxygen's worst window at 276 umol against its bound of 4. This ticket's measured day replaces them.

From [ticket 10](10-lungs-breathe.md): nothing refuses inspired oxygen and carbon dioxide shares that sum above one, which would make nitrogen gas's inspired share negative. When this ticket states the room's gas as inputs, it holds that sum.

From [ticket 10](10-lungs-breathe.md)'s last PR: when the day states the room's humidity and pressure, the lining and the expired water's route read them, and `rs.room-humidity` goes, with `rs.room-pressure` from 10a, so one fact has one source. The day's water intake also covers the lungs' 364.1 units a day; until it does, admission balances that loss with less urine.

From the measurement before [native-contract 85](../../native-contract/issues/85-the-certificates-windows-end-on-breath-edges.md)'s second PR: a day of the whole body's complete links is about 1.1 TB compacted, more than the build worker that measured it has free, and certifying a day from a disk store would take about 6.8 hours at 1.47 ms a link. So this ticket waits on [native-contract 95](../../native-contract/issues/95-the-record-trims-its-history.md), which waits on [native-contract 94](../../native-contract/issues/94-the-certificate-folds-as-the-run-goes.md): the fold judges the day as the run goes, and 95 says where the rolling day of the body's links is kept.

From the same measurement, on a run of 12,600 s at main `e2d5c6ba`, awake to 1,800 s, asleep to 9,000 s and awake after: none of the rows it followed was steady over those three and a half hours. Awake before sleep, energy gains 66.7 a second, oxygen 2.93 and carbon 1.43. Asleep they gain 22.1, 1.74 and 0.85. After the waking they fall, by 5.06, 2.72 and 1.33. The three salts gain a little at first and then fall for the rest of the run. Carried to 24 h at the first awake rate, energy's gain would be 5.8e6: an extrapolation, and no measured day. Each belongs to a loop that a ticket this one waits on still holds open: heat and the fuels (12 to 16), water and salt (17). The drive scale does not settle awake in 30 minutes, 1.038 rising to 1.064 as ten-minute means, and after the waking it falls from 1.170 to 1.090 by 12,600 s; asleep it settles by about 6,000 s. So this ticket's certificate box is where the 24 h bounds are first sized, from the measured day. Its PR also sizes the rates between breath edges again and says, for each of native-contract 85's old leak sizes (oxygen 20 a second, nitrogen gas 20, carbon 9, vasopressin 3e-4), whether the day's rates refuse it, and it runs that ticket's open item 3, a leak while the rate moves.
