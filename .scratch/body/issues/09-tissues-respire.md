## The four PRs

The ticket lands as four PRs: 09a the burn, 09b lactate and the oxygen debt, 09c arrow 90, 09d arrow 78. The plan and its prototypes are outside the repo.

- 09d waits on a contract change: the heart's and the vessel walls' work start at the exterior's chemical energy, and no route family starts at a bed's heat store.
- Research 11's debt in memory cannot be stated, because memory is body-wide only. So 09b reads the debt from the cells' lactate.
- The oxygen rate is research 11's smooth minimum less its value at no cell oxygen, `smooth_min(D, k P, s D) - smooth_min(D, 0, s D)`, with the width a share `s` of the demand. A review of nightingale#180 found the plain form below zero there, and on the one-bed fixture with the cell membrane shut and the cells at 0 mmHg it ran both burns backward at up to 9.4e-3 oxygen a second, raising the cells to 2e-3 mmHg. So the rate is zero at a cell partial pressure of zero, and it passes the demand by at most that offset, `D (sqrt(1 + s^2) - 1) / 2`, which is 1e-4 of the demand at `s` = 0.02. 09b's debt form takes the same offset.
- 09b writes the oxygen rate as two smooth maxima, `B = smooth_max(D, 0, s D) - smooth_max(D - k P, 0, s D)`, the same function. A second break-it pass on nightingale#180 found two edges of the form with smooth minima. At a demand of 1e-170 the squares underflowed and the rate read -5e-171, so the burns ran backward, and a run there faulted at 8,033 s once the bed's heat was spent. With `k P` at 1e20 times the demand the smooth minimum lost the demand to rounding, and the bed burned 0.009999 of a demand of 100. In the new form neither smooth maximum loses its operand: the tiny demand burns 5e-171, half its demand, because its squares still underflow, and the abundant reach burns 100.01. Both runs are tests on the fixture, and a mutant row puts the old form back. A third break-it pass on nightingale#182 found two edges this form keeps, both where squares underflow, under about 1e-154. At a demand of 1e-170 with the cells at 41.7 mmHg the bed burns 5.0e-171, half its demand, because the ceiling's square underflows. At a demand of zero and a reach of 1e-170 a mmHg the oxygen rate reads 2.07e-169 and glycolysis -1.73e-170, because the shortfall's square underflows and the exact maximum `0.5 (x + sqrt(x^2))` with it. Neither can be closed inside the contract's operators without a constant no packet entry backs (`UnbackedConstant`). The body's packet sits about 154 orders of magnitude from both: its least demand is 1 and its least reach 0.1 a mmHg, so its least reach times a cell pressure of 38 mmHg is 3.8.
- At the body's reach a bed keeps its oxygen rate at its demand down to half its flow and up to half again its demand, so halving a bed's flow raises its extraction and no debt. The PR that sends the debt to the vessels settles the reach, and the size of the signal, against the box on halving a bed's flow. On the one-bed fixture at the body's reach the debt first rises between one and a half and 1.8 times the demand (0.87 after two minutes at 1.8 times) and between three tenths and a quarter of the flow (0.014 at a quarter).
- The check of nightingale#182 found a third figure below the zeros' range, which holds exactly from a demand of 1e-150 up: at a demand of 1e-160 glycolysis reads -1.0e-165. No packet states a demand under 1, so none reaches these edges.
- 09b's debt is three times the cells' lactate, with no reference amount. The same pass showed a positive reference let the first order burn take the lactate below it and the debt below zero, which arrow 90's reader may not see. The body's reference was zero at rest, so dropping it changed no admitted value and no debt can be negative.
- 09b's glycolysis splits glucose into two lactate at `g = r max(D - B, 0)`, `r` = 1/6, exact so that it is zero wherever the oxygen rate meets the demand. Lactate burns at `l = k N P / (P + K)`, `k` = 0.02 a second and `K` = 2 mmHg, zero with no lactate and with no cell oxygen. Both forms are model defaults with no source; [research 17](../../research/issues/17-lactate-oxidation-and-yield.md) asks for them. Lactate holds 450 a unit, so glycolysis frees 100 a turnover into the bed's heat.
- The debt is metabolism's arrow 90 field, `d = 3 N`, defined at every bed and read by no law until 09c. At rest no bed splits glucose, because the body's reach meets the demand at 10 mmHg and every bed's cells sit at 38 mmHg or more, so each bed admits with no cell lactate and owes nothing. A burn of lactate above a resting amount was tried and dropped: with no glycolysis at rest it drained the lactate below that amount, so the debt never settled.
- Lactate is a strong anion and glucose has none, but no reaction moves a fixed charge and the cells have no acidity at rung 0, so glycolysis changes no conserved quantity's charge. On the fixture every quantity, charge with the elements and energy, closes to 1e-12 of its window while glycolysis runs.
