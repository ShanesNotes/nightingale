From 07: a channel names a row by `RowKind`, so a solute's row or an algebraic pressure reaches a lens with no change, and a field added to `RowKind` reaches `ChannelSpec` by itself. From 08: a packet states its goal with `ParameterPacket::periodic(cycle)`, and `admit_solved` solves it. Five things wait for this ticket. A kidney's rate that reads an amount also stops a total being neutral, so decide whether that total is still specified. `solve_dense` is the plain solve and costs the cube of points times rows: a whole admission took 11 s at 2,001 unknowns and 93 s at 4,001, so the slice needs an elimination that follows the block ring, behind `initialize`. An anchor can read an amount row, or a row that reads a drive, and per-site rows then pass `AnchorOnParameters` by themselves. When `ErrorScales` gets row scales, `InitializationSystem::held` takes the row, and the packet's `within` states them.
