## Answer

The slice's organ laws compile on the contract. The body starts at the 06:55 anchor on three anchors, runs on the reference core, closes every conserved quantity, and answers a stand through its own reflex. All values are synthetic.

- **The slice compiles with no law naming a site and no organ owning a pool.** Five law crates (`ng-cardiovascular`, `ng-fluids`, `ng-renal`, `ng-nervous`, `ng-receptor`) and the held cards give 240 rows and 256 columns. A law reads a port and the map says where. Pools belong to the schema. The E0603 probe lands in `ng-cardiovascular` and the E0451 probe in `ng-receptor`, each with a legal twin. There is no reaction type: a binding is a paired transfer.
- **Initialization meets every fixture anchor with recorded provenance.** Heart rate, mean arterial pressure and filtration each have a fit: the heart's own rate, the renal afferent resistance and the tubule's sodium capacity. Provenance names the anchor behind each fit, says which start values were solved and which came from the fixture, and gives the row nearest its limit. The solve takes about 17 s in a release build.
- **Water, ions, moieties and energy close over the first accepted steps with the reference core.** A 2 s run through at least eight valve and rhythm firings closes every conserved quantity to one part in 1e12 of what crossed it, charge included. The elastic store stays on the sum of the walls. Held pools do not move.

`crates/ng-validate/tests/arena_body.rs` holds these checks in one test on one solve, each from a checkpoint of the start. It also checks that the body regulates itself:

- Every link of every loop must move its reader the way the loop needs, on the admitted start, 49 links in all. A reversed law closes every conserved quantity and still admits, so a slope is not enough. The loops are the baroreflex from pressure to transmitter to rate, wall and arteriole in both ventricles, feedback at the kidney, Starling and lymph at every bed with what encloses it, and vasopressin from sodium to water back from the duct.
- A stand runs through the reflex. Blood falls into the legs, the sinus feels less stretch, traffic rises by more than a fifth and the rate follows. No law reads posture but the heads. Each sensor feels the column up to its own height.
- A stand and a sit settle the way the contract's 07:00 case checks, by direction: at heart level the pressure settles above supine, the rate rises, stroke volume and output fall, and filtration falls a little. Traffic stays under 0.8, so the reflex has room left, and beat means swing under 1, so the loop does not hunt.
- A meal's sodium, potassium and chloride reach the blood. A 400 ml bolus over a second keeps the body running.
- In late diastole the heat shed matches what the flows lose to within a tenth, so the rate that closes the store is a real account of dissipation.
- A checkpoint in mid beat, with a posture input after it, runs on to the same bits.

**Choices, each with its reason.**

- **Two designs were built and judged.** The port kept the smaller one, which already admitted the whole slice. The judge preferred the other design's rules, so each of its counterexamples became a fix here and a test, and so did each of the Codex adversarial pass's eight. The lines below say what stands now.
- **One firing rule.** The run and the ring fire guards through one function. Of two guards on one variable the first in stable order wins, and a guard a firing arms waits for the next step.
- **A crossing inside a step refuses the step.** A guard crossed and crossed back leaves no mark at the step's end, so every stage before the end is read, one guard at a time, and a shorter step lands on it. Another guard's crossing at the end hides nothing. At the floor the declared resolution is the limit. No check can prove nothing happened between the points a step looks at.
- **No solve at a landing.** No algebraic law may hold by a mode, read a row a firing resets, hazards' included, or read a draw a firing moves, so no algebraic row jumps. A valve's flow is its memory, so a reset leaves nothing stale, and a reset's row must hold zero. The dissipated power is the one row that shows a left limit at a firing, because it is a rate.
- **A valve opens past a small head.** It shuts at or below zero head and flow, as research 02 says, and opens only past `cv.opening-head`, so a still body fires nothing.
- **A map states what is absent.** A law that reads a junction at a site needs a supply or `AnatomyBuilder::unsupplied`, and a junction needs the receptor. A lost line is `NoSupply` or `NoReceptor`, and never reads as no effect.
- **A route states what it stops.** A species one end of a fluid route lacks is `CargoStranded` until the route says `keeps_back`, and a species a route keeps back stays back even when both ends hold it.
- **A total is solved only if the body regulates it.** A boundary law that reads the body makes a total regulated, and admission solves it. That test reads syntax, so `admit` also reads every conserved quantity's net flow over the cycle and the rank of the whole system, fits included. The ring solve moved into the contract so that `admit` can run it. A pivot is read beside its row and column as given, because after elimination a row's own largest entry can be rounding.
- **Energy has one store for the walls.** The heart's work and gravity's arrive as receipts, and a dissipated power moves the store into heat. That rate is what keeps the store on the sum of the walls, and its closure is judged at the store's own scale. The moving blood's energy and the stretch of tissue and tubule are not in the sum, so they pass through it as heat.
- **The energy account never stops a body.** Water entering a wall and a vein's muscle tightening have no receipt, so for that moment heat reads low. Below zero that is a validity excursion on the step, not a refusal. Pricing each with its own receipt would add a route per cause, and an account should not be able to end a run.
- **Traffic rests low on its curve.** A body at the middle of the logistic can only double its traffic, and a stand spent all of it. One resting shift per sigmoid leaves room, so a stand holds pressure at about two thirds of full traffic.
- **Norepinephrine clears slowly and the filter is quick.** The gain a stand needs rang at a 4.6 s period on lags of a second, with pressure swinging 95 to 117. Research 02 lets the junction pool supply the lag, and its check has the conductance low at the sixth cycle, so the slow step is at the junction and the loop settles in about eight beats.
- **Posture is a trunk and legs.** Heights run from the hip. A ramp is a train of events.
- **Point 0 is the first stage after the wrap,** so the phase there is one interval past zero. The period stays fixed and a fit on the heart's own rate lands the wrap on the ring's last point, so no period unknown is needed.
- **Small ions cross a capillary wall on their own,** `fl.ion-exchange`. Water alone left tissue sodium a nearly free level, three times the blood's, and Newton could not find it.
- **Albumin leaks across every capillary wall,** `fl.albumin-leak`, by the same law at a far lower rate. Water only ever leaves the peritubular interstitium toward the capillary, so nothing fed its albumin, and Newton stalled on that nearly free amount for harmless changes to the table. With a source, the solve is sturdier and quicker.
- **A vessel below its unstressed volume collapses and sucks nothing in, and the leg veins have a valve.** Without them a stand ran a chamber dry.
- **Sodium reclaim saturates.** A fixed share leaves the sodium the macula densa sees blind to filtration, so the feedback has nothing to read.
- **The solver's own work.** Admission marches 40 beats, then runs Newton on the ring with a line search, then sweeps the guards. The ring update walks the fixed elimination and solves one dense corner.

**Findings.** Finding 14: `deviation` returns `Deviation<U>`, which leaves only over a width of its own dimension or as a set point's rate. Finding 17: a ticket now stops at the body's tightest rate class and at a forcing's knot. On the slice that cost is less than nothing: asked for 20 ms steps, the capped run took 3.9 s for 5 simulated seconds and an uncapped copy took 4.9 s, because long steps are refused and solved again, and its pressure sat further from the anchor. That was measured before the last change to the table. The cap is 10 ms, the wave class of the heart's laws. A span past a law's interval is also how a core could see past a breakpoint, so the cap narrows that too.

**Speed.** A stage's residual, slopes, factor and solve take about 140 microseconds on 240 rows, and a ring update takes about 150 ms on 24,000 unknowns. The reference core takes 2.2 ms a step at 1 ms steps and runs at 1.3 times real time at 5 to 10 ms steps. The cost is the three stage solves of a doubled step, the landings at about ten firings a beat, and the record's own work. That is a first order reference, not the arena's bar, and it says what a faster core must beat. The reverse sweep and the fixed elimination are what bring it here: forward slopes and a dense solve cost about ten times as much.
