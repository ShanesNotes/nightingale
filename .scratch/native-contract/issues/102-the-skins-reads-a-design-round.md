**What to build.** The contract edits of [record 0011](../../../docs/adr/0011-blood-carries-heat-into-the-store-a-bed-sits-in-and-each-card-reads-through-a-grant.md), in one PR that lands dormant. The record is the design: read it whole first. Each box below names the consequence it builds as (0011, n). Each of the record's 46 consequences is a box here or a non-goal below, and the last two boxes (the glossary's entries for the record's terms and the PR's own, and the merge proof) are the repo's rules and outside that count. The new interfaces are exercised in synthetic test assemblies, and every value they bind is obviously synthetic. No production law card, anatomy or packet changes. The names of public items below are the design sketch's.

**Why.** PR 12b must carry heat to the skin at the skin bed's own flow, sense the skin's temperature at the thermoreceptor, narrow and widen the skin arteriole, and make sweat. NS releases acetylcholine at the skin junction, which supplies the skin bed. MT computes the sweat rate at the skin bed. The water and salts leave the skin bed's interstitium, and the heat leaves the skin heat site's store, the store the skin thermoreceptor reads. On main none of these reads compiles. This PR builds the contract's part of each. PR 12b then reparents the skin bed, adds the skin junction's acetylcholine pools with their release, reuptake and receptor bindings, binds the capacities, and moves the production CV, NS and MT laws onto the grants and MT's conduction onto the published temperatures (0011, 41 to 44).
