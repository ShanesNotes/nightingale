# 104: What no test of the body's policy holds yet

From: the checks of nightingale#222 and nightingale#224, [native-contract 85](85-the-certificates-windows-end-on-breath-edges.md)'s second PR and [native-contract 100](100-the-body-moves-after-admission-what-moves-and-why.md)'s fix

Spec: `body_certificate` in `crates/ng-validate/src/body/packet.rs`, `certificate_with` in `crates/ng-validate/src/slice.rs`, and `RULE` and the leak tests in `crates/ng-validate/tests/body_run.rs`

Type: task

Status: open

Blocked by: none

Blocks: none

**Why.** I19 holds the whole body for 48 hours under its certificate, and the body's policy is only as strong as the tests that would see it change. The checks of the two PRs that built and re-sized that policy found five things no test holds, and a sixth that a test holds with no mutant row. Every value is synthetic.

- [ ] Each fixed drift bound of the body's policy that native-contract 85's table does not rate is under a rule test, or carries its reason. Today `assert_body_rule` in `crates/ng-validate/tests/body_run.rs` holds the rated rows only: it skips a conserved quantity no row of `RULE` holds once it finds it unrated, so it does not see a change to an unrated bound. A check's test changed albumin's fixed bound from 1 to 2 in a policy built as `rule_refuses_the_bodys_policy_with_windows_one_nanosecond_longer` builds one, and the rule test accepted it: the gap it shows is in the rule test alone. Two other tests reach an unrated bound: `the_bodys_own_drift_bounds_are_its_stated_ones` in `crates/ng-validate/tests/body.rs` compares each compiled bound with a separately stated policy, and a change to the slice's bound for an unmoved species also reaches `norepinephrine_drifts_up_to_the_floor_of_a_quantity_the_slice_does_not_move` in `crates/ng-validate/tests/arena_certificate.rs`. The unrated fixed bounds, counted over the body's 398 compiled conserved combinations against `RULE` and `certificate_with`: 50 are rated; 347 are of a species `SPECIES_BOUNDS` does not name, and each takes the slice's bound for an unmoved species, `UNMOVED_DRIFT`, 1, among them albumin, the transmitters, every hormone but vasopressin, and carbon dioxide as a species; and charge has no drift bound. No combination of the body takes `SYNTHETIC_DRIFT`, 1e4, the fallback for an element `ELEMENT_BOUNDS` does not name
- [ ] Sodium, chloride and potassium each have a leak test on the body, as the four leaks of `Leak::ALL` (oxygen, carbon, nitrogen gas and vasopressin) do. Native-contract 100's fix loosened their bounds, each the ten-second bound and the edge rate: sodium from 2e-3 and 2e-4 to 5e-3 and 5e-4, chloride from 2e-3 and 2e-4 to 6e-3 and 6e-4, and potassium from 9e-5 and 8e-6 to 3e-4 and 3e-5 (`SPECIES_BOUNDS`, on main before nightingale#224 at `dfc9cf05` and on main after it). No test shows that the policy refuses a salt leak of a stated size. The four leaks' helper, `Leak::quantity`, needs one conserved combination for each leak and panics otherwise, and the body has 13 sodium combinations, 13 potassium combinations and two chloride combinations. So each salt's test names the conserved combination its injected route reaches and asserts a refusal on that combination at a stated net rate
- [ ] A body test reads the coronary bed's lymph against its admitted rate, and says for the gut's and the liver's whether it reads them or why not. Today the suite reads only the pulmonary lymph (`the_lungs_interstitial_water_and_lymph_keep_their_admitted_rates_over_the_awake_minutes_breaths`), so a threshold left wrong at another enclosed bed would be caught only by the identity pins. The coronary bed is the one other bed the breath swings
- [ ] A body that declares a rhythm is judged by a policy with windows between edges, or this ticket states why nothing enforces it. `certificate_with` and `CertificatePolicy::between_edges` are public, so a caller can build a policy with no edges or with fewer rates, as the leak test's `Leak::alone` does, and nothing refuses a body that declares a rhythm under a policy with none
- [ ] `the_lungs_interstitial_water_and_lymph_keep_their_admitted_rates_over_the_awake_minutes_breaths` states its window so that the solver's last step cannot change which breaths it scores. Today the window runs from the first breath edge to the last edge any accepted step of the minute reached, the minute ends on the first accepted step at or past 60 s, and the longest step is 10 ms, so the count rises by one when an edge falls in those 10 ms. The suite's own run is not such a run: its eleven breaths end at 59.431 s. A check's run with a second awake input 1 ns before 60 s has its last accepted step land on the edge at 60.000900406 s: ten breaths end by 60 s, at 55.000850407 s, and the test scores eleven. Both windows pass on that run, the lung's water at +4.930e-5 a second over eleven and +4.912e-5 over ten against 2e-4, and the lymph's difference from its admitted rate at +2.828e-4 and +2.710e-4 against 6e-4
- [ ] `EDGE_WINDOW` in `crates/ng-validate/src/body/packet.rs`, the certificate's ten-second window length, has a mutant row in `tools/gate/mutants.toml`. Today `each_figure_of_rule_is_the_bodys_policy_bit_for_bit` holds it, since it checks the body's policy against a window of ten seconds, and no row mutates it, while the settling time has its row, `body-settling-time-apart-from-rule`, which names that test. `rule_refuses_the_bodys_policy_with_windows_one_nanosecond_longer` does not hold it: it adds one nanosecond to whatever length the policy has, so it still panics as it expects when the length changes

**Notes.**

- A leak present from admission is taken into the admitted state, so the body's leak tests show the refusal of a leak that starts after admission, not of one present at it: under the mutant row `leak-gate-left-open` the shut branch carries every leak from admission and still passes each leak's own policy over 40 s.
- A check measured the four leaked quantities, oxygen, carbon, nitrogen gas and vasopressin, on held-rate controls: at a breath rate of 0.20 a second each one's drift has the opposite sign to its drift at 0.25, so native-contract 85's "against its drift" holds for the leak test's signs at 0.25 only. The same check refused all 16 cases, both directions at both rates, with each leaking branch judged under its own leak's policy, `Leak::alone`, with one edge rate; only the controls passed the body's whole policy (ticket 85, "What the second PR built").
- [Body ticket 12](../../body/issues/12-heat-balance.md) holds that the leaking body's gate reads the room's humidity, which 12a's evaporation law reads too, so the gate moves before that law lands.
- [Body ticket 36](../../body/issues/36-the-whole-body-answers-posture.md) holds what a posture change does to the certificate: the body with no leak is refused at 75 s, and a vasopressin leak of 3e-3 a second is certified through 70 s.
- [Body ticket 28](../../body/issues/28-two-synthetic-days.md) holds that no 48-hour protocol uses the body's policy yet.

**Non-goals:** the rated bounds' figures, which stay native-contract 85's and are sized by its rule, and a cause for the salts' drift, which is native-contract 100's open box on the admitted beat and body ticket 17's.
