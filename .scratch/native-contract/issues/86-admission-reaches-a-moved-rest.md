# 86: Admission reaches a moved rest, and settles near-coincident crossings

Spec: [solver contract](../../../docs/architecture/solver-contract.md), section 4, `crates/ng-contract/src/admission.rs` and `crates/ng-validate/src/initialize.rs`

Type: prototype

Status: open

Blocked by: none

**Why.** The first milestone (SRS invariant I19) runs the whole body for 48 hours, and every rung that moves the breathing body's rest meets admission first, controlled breathing first. [Native-contract 82](82-what-a-moved-rest-does-not-admit.md) found two limits of the admission section 4 states. Neither is shown to be the physiology, and the first is not Newton's conditioning.

- **A sweep that cycles.** With the pleural pressure read from the lung's volume, the body has a state admission accepts: solved from the static form's state, it admits in 55 to 58 s. From the packet's guess it never does. Two valve closings sit 4 microseconds apart in one interval. Newton's update would carry one past the other, admission keeps the crossings of an interval in order, so the update is clamped, Newton stops at the clamp, and the sweep drops the crossing and finds it again, until the sweep limit.
- **A guess that cannot reach a moved rest.** With the rest drive at the breath's mean and the chest wall's rest unfitted, Newton from the packet's guess stalls. A continuation in the rest drive admits each step to 95% of the mean, with more updates at each step, and the last step had not settled after 140 updates. Two rules of the solve refused steps that more work admits: it stops when Newton has used its 32 updates unsettled and the sweep finds no change, and it takes a trial that raises the merit when none lowers it.

**What body 11's plan measured.** [Body ticket 11](../../body/issues/11-breathing-is-controlled.md) does not wait on this ticket: its controller scales no rest, so both valve closings are main's to every digit, and the body admitted from the packet's guess with the controller in it in every form tried. Under a controller that did scale the rest, the closings came close or crossed, and each run admitted. Main has the leg vein's closing 41.5 microseconds after the aortic valve's. With the drive scale on the breath's depth the leg vein's closed 28.1 microseconds before it, and 276 before at four times the gain. With depth through the pump's junction it sat 3.2 microseconds after it, inside the 4 at which native-contract 82's sweep cycled. The cause was the drive scale's beat ripple reaching the rest drive, which moved 0.033%. This ticket stays the fix for any later move of the admitted rest.

**What body ticket 10's last PR found.** The lung's water slowed admission with no shown cause in its own rows. An independent check counted, back to back at the base and with the water: ring Newton 24 to 39 iterations, 2,280.6 to 3,130.1 G instructions, 155.5 to 231.8 s, the march unchanged (10 calls, 58 beats, leg Newton 90,683 and 91,286), and ruled out by run the gas water's guess and the kidney's rebalance. A probe printed, for each ring iteration, the slot whose relative move sets the step and the merit. Both reach a merit of about 2e-26 in one Newton pass of 23 iterations. The base stops there. With the water, the sweeps then relocate valve crossings (the aortic valve at points 80 and 106, the mitral at 112, the tricuspid at 4 and 106), and three more short passes follow, each led by a valve memory or the right ventricle's pressure. No slot the water moved sets a step. So the time goes to settling crossings that the water's small shift of the circulation moved near a grid point, which this ticket's first box asks about.

**What the round decides.** Each is settled by two or three prototypes on the whole body, with the volume read and the moved rest as its cases.

- [ ] How the solve settles two located crossings in one interval whose order Newton would swap: reorder them, merge them into one knot, or relocate them from the new curves rather than clamp. The volume read admits from the packet's guess
- [ ] How admission reaches a rest the packet's guess cannot: a continuation in a packet value from a solved body, or a guess solved from another form. What the start state's identity covers when its guess came from another solve, given that `admit` stays the only judge
- [ ] When the solve stops: whether an unsettled Newton with no change in the sweep ends the solve, and whether a trial that raises the merit may stand
- [ ] Whether the rest drive at the breath's mean has a state at all: the continuation from 0.3513 to 0.368401 settles, or a turning point is shown
- [ ] The cost of each choice in admission's time, against today's 240 s from the packet's guess

**Non-goals:** the breath's rhythm, the static pleural form and the fitted rest the body states today.

## Comments

From the solver explorations (evidence in `~/lanes/logs/ng-explore-solver/` and `~/lanes/logs/ng-solver-exploration-5fe4e63/`), three inputs to the round. None was run on the whole body.

- **The curve the scan reads leaves the range of its points.** A leg's dense curve is the quadratic through its start, its stage at a third and its end (`admission/crossings.rs`). Through 1, 3 and 3 that is `-6s^2 + 8s + 1`, which peaks at 3.667 at two thirds of the leg. A row that relaxes inside the first third bulges by a third of its jump, and a guard at 3.2 reads two crossings its points never made. Two explorations found it separately, neither rerun: one staged a row that follows a drive from 1 to 3 with a guard at 3.2 and was refused as `Inconsistent`, and one read the curve at -0.226 between stages that were all positive. For the first box (inferred): two closings a few microseconds apart can be the two roots of a bulge, which appear and vanish as Newton moves the stages, and a relocation "from the new curves" inherits it. One prototype reads the guards on a curve held inside its points' range and counts the sweeps.
- **The merit weighs an anchor by its fit's size.** A fitted slot's share is its residual over one plus the size of the fitted unknown (`size` gives 1.0 for a fitted slot), and an anchor's residual is the read less the anchor's value, in the anchor's unit. The anchor's own size never enters. One exploration staged an anchor of 1e-12 and was refused as `Inconsistent` where the same fixture at unit size admitted. Its merit scaled by the anchor's value admitted that fixture and took `admission_energy` from 9.9 s to 18.7 s in one run, because the stage rows then outweigh the anchors (`~/lanes/logs/ng-explore-solver/report-2/claims.tsv`, rows F6 and FIX-F6, not rerun). The third box's "a trial that raises the merit" reads this weighting, so the scaling is its question and not a one-line change.
- **The ring may already hold what the fourth box asks** (inferred from the ring's form, not run). With the legs eliminated, the corner carries a change at the cycle's start around one cycle. It loses rank where that map has a multiplier of one, which is a turning point, and the largest multiplier says whether a run that starts near the admitted cycle stays on it and how many cycles it takes to settle. Admission reads neither today. One exploration admitted a rest that a run leaves, and met `NoRank` on a growth of 12 a second over 0.5 s legs, where the two-stage Radau IIA factor `(1 + z/3) / (1 - 2z/3 + z^2/6)` is exactly one at `z = 6`. That case is the stage scheme's own: past `z = 6` it reads a fast growth as a decay, so a multiplier from the ring holds only for a mode slower than six over a leg.

The second box's question, what the start state's identity covers when its guess came from another solve, is also the question for a guess a learned model proposes ([generative physiology map](../../generative-physiology/map.md), not yet specified).

Start proposals are the earliest build in that line, so this ticket's second box also decides how a proposed start travels for replay across the three routes in fork 1 (packet start entries, a host-filled public `AdmissionWorkspace`, or a validation-private solve).

From a review of [generative-physiology 18](../../generative-physiology/issues/18-teacher-library-and-start-proposals.md) (section 2.1), which ran each of those routes at `9a7469ff` on the compiled whole body, with no admission solve (measured; `admission.rs` and `crossings.rs` line numbers refreshed to `5b554ecf`):

- **Packet start entries.** `body_packet` already starts every row and mode (`crates/ng-validate/src/body/packet.rs:683-706`), a second start is refused as `DuplicateStart` (`crates/ng-contract/src/packet.rs:599`), and `packet_of` is private (`body/packet.rs:602`). So a host cannot put another start on the whole body's packet without a builder from this crate. Varied packets need the same builder ([generative-physiology 15](../../generative-physiology/issues/15-learned-emulator-for-parameters.md), Comments).
- **A host-filled workspace.** A fresh workspace holds no crossings (`crates/ng-contract/src/admission.rs:844-857`), so `shift_crossing` returns `CrossingOutside` until `sweep` has run (`crates/ng-contract/src/admission/crossings.rs:599`), and unknowns taken from a solve that has crossings do not fit it. A workspace also cannot pass between two packets: the system's identity seals the packet's digest, and `admit` refuses another system's workspace as `ForeignWorkspace` (`admission.rs:275-279, 627-636`). The whole body's workspace holds 293,003 unknowns over 100 points.
- **This crate's solve.** `initialize` is private (`crates/ng-validate/src/initialize.rs:243`). `solve_fixture` is public and returns the system with its solved workspace (`initialize.rs:416`), so a host can already join it to the second route.

That line's start proposals wait for this ticket's pick and then need one ingress function for the picked route.
