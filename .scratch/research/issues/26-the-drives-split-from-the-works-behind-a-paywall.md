**Question:** What do the works below give toward these four boxes, once obtained? Research 23's Answer holds what the works on hand give toward each.

- [ ] Rate and tidal volume against ventilation over hypercapnic and hypoxic steps from rest, with the population and the method. Research 23 has a hypercapnic ramp under hyperoxia (Clark and von Euler 1972) and no hypoxic step. A primary study of rate and tidal volume under an isocapnic hypoxic step would settle the hypoxic half, and one under a hypercapnic step from rest the other
- [ ] Whether the split differs between the central and the peripheral arm, or between waking and NREM sleep. Research 23 has no split by arm and none under a chemical step in sleep. A method that separates the arms' components, such as Dahan 1990's, applied to rate and tidal volume under an isocapnic hypoxic step and a hyperoxic CO2 step would settle the arms; Gothe 1981's full text could settle sleep
- [ ] Each constant's row for [research 16](16-constants-and-what-binds-them.md)'s list: what measurable quantity binds it. Research 23 proposes the rows for its form; the rows behind resting tidal volume and resting rate wait on [research 20](20-the-breaths-rhythm.md), for which Tobin 1983 could give the resting means
- [ ] What bounds depth as the drive rises, and where rate carries the rest of the rise, with the population and the method. Research 23 has no measured bound under a chemical drive. Hey 1966, or a later work that restates it, could give one

## The works, each with what it would settle

Not had, behind a paywall; a later work stands in where it restates one, at its own tier:

- Hey 1966, Respiration Physiology 1:193 to 205 (checked in Clark and von Euler's reference list, p. 294, and its Crossref record): tidal volume against ventilation under its stimuli, its constants and the volume where depth stops rising, for research 23's linear form and the first and fourth boxes here. Clark and von Euler restate its shape, ventilation linear in tidal volume (p. 291), and what its examples showed (p. 293); no page on hand gives a constant or a plateau. Its Crossref, Europe PMC and OpenAlex records hold no abstract. Two later works said to restate it could not be had: Tipton 2017 (J Physiol 595:5729, PMC5577533), whose Europe PMC full-text service answered with a server error and whose author manuscript at its university's research portal answered with a browser challenge; and Salazar-Martínez 2024 (Adv Respir Med 92(1):58 to 65, doi:10.3390/arm92010008, a study of breathing in exercise by its title), whose publisher's article page and PDF link refused the request. A read of Hey, or of either, could supply `h_V` or the plateau, and where Hey's runs began, and so could decide between research 23's two forms.
- Milic-Emili and Grunstein 1976, Chest 70 suppl:131 to 133 (Douglas 1982's reference 28, printed p. 844): Douglas cites it for mean inspiratory flow and inspiration's share of the breath (printed p. 843). No page on hand gives its own equations or calibrates the engine's drive scale to that flow, which is research 23's choice 1; a read of it could support or refute that choice.
- Gothe 1981, J Appl Physiol 50:724 to 730 (checked against the registry's Crossref record, which holds the abstract research 23 cites): rate and tidal volume under CO2 awake and in stage II sleep, if its results give them, for the second box.
- Tobin 1983, Chest 84:202 to 205 (checked by the curation study against its PubMed record, whose abstract says the components were measured and gives no means): the resting means in normal subjects, tidal volume, rate, inspiration's length and the breath's length, for research 20's boxes and so the third box. On hand for research 20 to weigh: Stradling's awake tidal volume and rate in six men (p. 366), and Motamedi-Fakhr's rate and timings without volume (p. 6).

Still a model default, each with research 23's search that found no source: the hypoxic step's split; the arms' split; the split under CO2 in sleep; and any bound on depth under a chemical drive.
