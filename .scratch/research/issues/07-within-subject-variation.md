# 07: Within-subject variation

Type: research
Status: open
Blocked by: 01

**Question:** What is the within-subject variation of each anchor reading by its method and window, and of each conserved quantity's amount?

- [ ] One record per anchor reading, for the return check
- [ ] One record per conserved quantity, for the certificate
- [ ] An amount no method can measure rests on an inference record reasoning from sourced flows (record 0004)

[Body ticket 17](../../body/issues/17-volume-hormones.md)'s day run and [body ticket 24](../../body/issues/24-the-synthetic-day.md) need reviewed bounds for the salts' stores, water and arterial carbon dioxide, and no record in `data/` gives them. The salts' stores and water are conserved quantities, which the certificate judges over sliding 24 h windows. Arterial carbon dioxide is an anchor reading, which the return check judges at the anchor's time of day. Body ticket 24 sizes its synthetic bounds on its measured day, and this ticket replaces them with reviewed ones.
