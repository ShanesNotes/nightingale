# 25: The peripheral chemoreflex on oxygen partial pressure

Type: research
Status: open
Blocked by: 11

**Question:** What form gives the peripheral chemoreflex's response on arterial oxygen partial pressure, which the carotid body senses, in place of saturation? [Research 11](11-law-forms-gas-loop.md)'s peripheral row reads `1 - S_a`, so [body ticket 11](../../body/issues/11-breathing-is-controlled.md)'s sensor reads arterial saturation. The two part when the dissociation curve shifts or a hemoglobin cannot carry oxygen, so the ticket that moves the curve revisits the reader.

Today's form: `u_p = G_p (1 - S_a) smooth_max(PaCO2 - B_p, 0, w)`, read at the sampled systemic arteries.

- [ ] A form on arterial oxygen partial pressure with carbon dioxide's interaction, with the source and the range it was fitted over
- [ ] Whether it meets Weil's and Douglas's healthy data as well as the saturation form does
- [ ] How it behaves in hyperoxia against Dahan's hyperoxic slopes, where research 11's form cuts the gain by more than 90%
- [ ] Each constant's row for [research 16](16-constants-and-what-binds-them.md)'s list: what measurable quantity binds it

**Non-goals:** the gains' values ([research 22](22-the-chemoreflex-gains-awake-and-asleep.md)), and disease of the carotid body.
