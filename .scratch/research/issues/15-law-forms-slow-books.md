## Answer

Each row is a form, where its constants go and where it holds, to the standard of [ticket 02](02-law-forms.md). Constants become records under [Parameters](05-parameters.md), and none is bound here. `Holds for` cites a source where one states a range and says "model default" where none was found. "Model adaptation" means a sourced form was changed to fit the contract, and the row says how. The rows are the ones that [body ticket 01](../../body/issues/01-rung-0-specification.md)'s tables give tickets 19 to 22, and no more.

Each source says how far it was read. "Full text" means the whole work was on hand in the evidence store's extracted text and the cited parts were read there. "Abstract" means the store does not hold the work and only its abstract was read, from Europe PMC. "Page image" means the page was read in the original file. No cited page is one the store marks as OCR. The store records no printed page label for Tietz's Textbook of Laboratory Medicine 7e or Ganong's Review of Medical Physiology 26e, so their pages are PDF pages. The curation fills and the healthy-body draft were leads and are cited nowhere.

In the forms, `N` is an amount in a pool, `c` a concentration, `W` the water a space holds and `GFR` the filtration rate. `relax(a, b, k)` is `k (a - b)`, so `relax(N, 0, k)` is a loss of `k N`, the first-order form the slice uses for a transmitter's reuptake (`crates/ng-nervous/src/lib.rs`). `E` is ticket 02's receptor effect at the site the row names. Zero is the contract's only literal, so each 1 in a form is a declared parameter or folds into one.

### The interfaces

With [research 13](13-law-forms-gut-and-fuels.md). Its gastric emptying fills the small intestine's lumen whose minerals and iron the absorption rows read, and its fecal exit carries their unabsorbed share and the shed iron. Its insulin effect at each bed moves phosphate into cells here as it moves potassium there. Its glucose output at the liver reads insulin's effect, whose efficacy cortisol lowers here.

With [research 14](14-law-forms-volume-and-acid.md). Its filtration, with the clock's gain, sets every filtered load here. In the strong-ion frame calcium and magnesium are strong cations and phosphate a weak acid whose total ticket 18 reads, and ionized calcium reads the pH that ticket 08 states. The urine its kidney makes enters the bladder here.

With [research 11](11-law-forms-gas-loop.md). Its oxygen content and hematocrit rows read hemoglobin per volume of blood, which follows dilution. The red cell row here moves that hemoglobin.

### Decisions

- **Saturable plus linear absorption for calcium and magnesium, linear for phosphate.** Stadt's model, after Peterson and Riggs, makes calcium and phosphate absorption linear in intake times a calcitriol factor (S1 Appendix Eqs B.2 and E.3). With calcitriol held, that absorbs a fixed fraction of any meal, where Heaney and Fine measured the fraction falling as the load rose. Phosphate has no healthy-adult fit on hand, and its passive path carries most of a meal's load (Modern Nutrition, Davis).
- **One shape for the three reclaims.** Each is a threshold per volume of filtrate, written with `smooth_min`, as Rude reports magnesium's maximum and as Guyton's phosphate threshold follows from its maximum and GFR. Stadt reclaims a fixed amount of phosphate per volume of filtrate (S1 Appendix Eqs E.4 and E.7), which is the same threshold without the floor at zero excretion. PTH moves only calcium's threshold, because body 01 gives PTH calcium alone. Guyton has PTH lowering phosphate's maximum too. ### What the contract lacks

Research 11's list stands. These are new:

- No operator turns the clock's phase into a periodic value. Arrow 89 types the circadian phase as `Phase` (`crates/ng-contract/src/catalog.rs`), and the cortisol profile needs the cosine and sine of it. Ticket 05 can deliver the clock as those two dimensionless inputs, as the boundary delivers posture as a sine. `Phase` states no zero, and Scheer counts from the core temperature minimum, so the clock also states its zero against that minimum. A second harmonic, if a later rung needs one, follows by `scale`, because `cos 2p = 2 cos^2 p - 1` and `sin 2p = 2 sin p cos p`, with the 2 and the 1 folded into declared coefficients.
- A void cannot be a delivery. A delivery carries an amount into the body at a rate its event sets (`crates/ng-contract/src/boundary.rs`), and the volume voided is a produced quantity. The void reaches the bladder's law as the gate `g`, held from one event to the next as posture is. No boundary input of that kind exists, and ticket 05 lists the void as an input of the day without its shape.
- The receptor layer's effect reads one ligand with a fixed efficacy for each site (`crates/ng-receptor/src/lib.rs`). Cortisol's row needs insulin's efficacy at the liver to fall with cortisol's lagged effect. Either the effect kernel reads a second input, or the layer publishes occupancy and HP's law finishes the effect. Ticket 20 picks.
- Iron absorption in the gut reads the store in the liver bed's cells, a pool at another site. Ticket 03's landmark list needs a place where GI's law reads that store, as the islets read glucose at the systemic arteries.
- Research 11 found that `Sensed` has no concentration. The PTH curve's set point is a calcium concentration, so it stays a declared parameter, not a set point.

No form needs a new operator. Each uses `relax`, `scale`, `multiply`, `divide`, `ln`, `logistic`, `smooth_max` or `smooth_min`.
