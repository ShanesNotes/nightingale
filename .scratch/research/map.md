# Research wayfinder map

## Destination

The reference man's reviewed packet and a reviewed healthy-range set exist as checked data in this repo, so [foundation ticket 01](../foundation/issues/01-arena-and-milestone.md) step 4 can build the milestone and [SRS invariant I19](../../docs/srs.md#4-invariants-each-becomes-a-test) can be judged. A fresh agent can bind any value in them without asking a question.

## Notes

- Tickets here are ordered by what each blocks first. Tickets 07 to 24 of native-contract run on synthetic numbers by type, so a missing number never holds them up. Only a law form can.
- Evidence ranks by SRS section 6: primary data from the target population, a compiled source, a textbook value, then inference. A value goes in at the best tier on hand, and its row says whether a primary source would upgrade it.
- A cross-check runs at two levels: inside one source for integrity, and across the packet's picks for fitness. A failure is explained by method, population or scale, or the value changes. Source strength never excuses a failed identity.
- Source text stays local. Only numbers and citations enter the repo.
- Pulse is a reference, never a pass bar (SRS rule 6).
- Tickets 11 to 15 give the law forms for the nine systems the slice lacks, one ticket for each loop of the [body map](../body/map.md).
- A search for a source starts in the curation study, the evidence store and the evidence library, which the [body map](../body/map.md)'s Notes locate. None of the three is a source.

## Decisions so far

- [Public data and the check over it](issues/01-data-layout-and-check.md): `data/` holds records as sorted JSON lines with closed fields and capped text, and `30-data` runs 18 rules and their must-fail probes over them. No crate reads `data/` yet.
- [Law forms the slice needs](issues/02-law-forms.md): the valve law is quadratic with inertance, resistance is linear in the alpha-1 effect, the effector's lag belongs to the junction pool, and viscosity takes Pries's in vivo law in discharge hematocrit. Two forms stand on open restatements and the venous gain on analogy until their primaries are read.
- [Law forms for the gas loop](issues/11-law-forms-gas-loop.md): 18 forms for body tickets 08 to 11. Blood carries both gases by Dash's equations, shifted by pH, PCO2 and temperature, with the 2,3-DPG factor held at 1. The peripheral chemoreflex reads desaturation, not Weil's hyperbola, and the controller's output is transmitter release. Four forms have no source on hand: force against drive, the vessels' answer to oxygen debt, the dry heat of warming inspired air and the breathing rhythm. It lists what the contract lacks for these forms, for body tickets 03, 09 and 11.
- [Law forms for heat](issues/12-law-forms-heat.md): 13 forms for body ticket 12, and sleep's gain on sympathetic traffic for body ticket 25. Heat sits in two stores, the thermostat sends one cholinergic command to sweat and skin dilation, and the clock moves only the set point, by two harmonics, while sleep steps it down. It leaves heat after a meal to research 13 and lists what the contract lacks for body tickets 03, 05 and 09: heat identity rows, the clock's cosine, a speed, and a transfer of each bed's heat to the core store.
- [Law forms for the gut and the fuels](issues/13-law-forms-gut-and-fuels.md): 32 forms for body tickets 13 to 16, with the meal's heat that research 12 left to it. The liver's glucose output is the sum of its reactions, insulin acts through each bed's interstitium and the receptor layer in place of each model's own pools, the stomach empties by what it holds, and a fecal exit tied to absorption closes every conserved quantity. It leaves residue units to body ticket 03, reaction grants for the hepatic and musculoskeletal cards to body ticket 04 and a gain on gluconeogenesis to body ticket 20, and lists what else the contract lacks: a rate of change, a value held from a delivery's instant and a place for the receptor layer to bind in a bed's interstitium.
- [Law forms for the volume hormones and the acid loop](issues/14-law-forms-volume-and-acid.md): 17 forms for body tickets 17 and 18. Every hormone acts through the receptor layer, pressure natriuresis stays a check while Hallow's factor acts on sodium reclaim, and acidity solves Antonius's strong ion balance with carbonate, hydroxide and hydrogen ion dropped. It leaves hemoglobin's charge to the ticket that states acidity, insulin's factor on the pump to research 13 and the minerals to research 15, and lists what the contract lacks for body tickets 03, 04 and 05.
- [Law forms for the slow books](issues/15-law-forms-slow-books.md): 19 forms for body tickets 19 to 22, and what coagulation and immunity cannot show while held. Each mineral has one exchangeable pool in bone and a threshold reclaim, cortisol follows one cosine of the clock and lowers insulin's efficacy at the liver, and a gate empties the bladder at each void. It leaves the clock's zero to body ticket 05, the receptor route to body ticket 20, and the iron landmark and magnesium's bone pool to body ticket 03.
- [The constants of rung 0 and what binds them](issues/16-constants-and-what-binds-them.md), the first part of [Parameters](issues/05-parameters.md): 556 constants, each beside the measurable quantity that would bind it. 410 bind from a measurement, 32 by derivation, 38 by inference record, 56 as model defaults, and 20 have no route yet. None is bound. Four kinds of constant cannot be bound as their forms are written: a produced quantity written as a constant, a set no measurement separates, a constant the contract cannot state yet, and a kept law with no form.
