# Research wayfinder map

## Destination

The reference man's reviewed packet and a reviewed healthy-range set exist as checked data in this repo, so [foundation ticket 01](../foundation/issues/01-arena-and-milestone.md) step 4 can build the milestone and [SRS invariant I19](../../docs/srs.md#4-invariants-each-becomes-a-test) can be judged. A fresh agent can bind any value in them without asking a question.

## Notes

- Tickets here are ordered by what each blocks first. Tickets 07 to 11 of native-contract run on synthetic numbers by type, so a missing number never holds them up. Only a law form can.
- Evidence ranks by SRS section 6: primary data from the target population, a compiled source, a textbook value, then inference. A value goes in at the best tier on hand, and its row says whether a primary source would upgrade it.
- A cross-check runs at two levels: inside one source for integrity, and across the packet's picks for fitness. A failure is explained by method, population or scale, or the value changes. Source strength never excuses a failed identity.
- Source text stays local. Only numbers and citations enter the repo.
- Pulse is a reference, never a pass bar (SRS rule 6).

## Decisions so far

- [Public data and the check over it](issues/01-data-layout-and-check.md): `data/` holds records as sorted JSON lines with closed fields and capped text, and `30-data` runs 18 rules and their must-fail probes over them. No crate reads `data/` yet.
- [Law forms the slice needs](issues/02-law-forms.md): the valve law is quadratic with inertance, resistance is linear in the alpha-1 effect, the effector's lag belongs to the junction pool, and viscosity takes Pries's in vivo law in discharge hematocrit. Two forms stand on open restatements and the venous gain on analogy until their primaries are read.

- Pharmacology and intervention numbers (scope stage 4).
