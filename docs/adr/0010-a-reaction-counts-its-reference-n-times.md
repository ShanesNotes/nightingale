# A reaction counts its reference species N times, used or made, and the contract divides its rate by N

A reaction counted its reference species once: `Proportions::<Q>::using` put `Q` at -1, the kernel returned `Rate<Q>`, and that value was the column's rate. Tickets 14 and 16 need four reactions that rule blocks or bends. Glycogen stored with a share of its glucose burned to pay for it, below one half in its own counts (20 glucose and 6 oxygen to 19 glycogen, 6 carbon dioxide and 25 water), has no species at count one, so it could not be one column. Urea from the amino acids and creatinine from body protein compiled only with the made species as the reference, written backward to run on a negative rate. Glucose from lactate, with its energy paid by burning fuel, had no forward form, and glycolysis run backward draws on the bed's heat.

Now the reference is any species the reaction uses or makes, at a whole count `N` of at least one. `Proportions::<Q, N>::using` puts it at `-N`, `Proportions::<Q, N>::making` at `+N`, and `N` is one unless stated, so every reaction written before keeps its proportions and its bytes. The kernel still returns `Rate<Q>`, the reference used or made per second, as a source states the rate. The contract divides that value by `N` where it becomes the column's rate, and where its slope against a row or a parameter is formed, so every term moves by its whole count times one extent, and that extent counts turnovers. The reference moves by the rate's integral, within the one rounding of the division. A count above one is sealed in the model hash after a tag, so a body that counts each reference once keeps its hash.

## Considered options

Each rule ran on a throwaway bench of the four reactions on one synthetic bed, 600 s at 10 s steps. The rule above is the bench's V4. In every run that compiled, every conserved quantity closed with a worst closure of 0, and each species moved by its count times the column's net extent, to 9 digits.

- The rule unchanged (V0): storage as two columns, a store and a burn whose rate is the store's times a share of 1/19, and urea, creatinine and glucose written backward, with the made species as the reference, on a negative rate. Every conserved quantity closed. Rejected: the share is a parameter nothing checks, and three of the four read inside out against their equations.
- `using_n::<N>`, with the rate counting turnovers (V1): all four forward. Rejected: a rate entered as the reference used per second, as a source gives it, ran the substrate N times fast, 19.17 times for N = 20, 1.99 for N = 2 and 2.98 for N = 3, and every conserved quantity still closed, so neither a compile check nor the certificate saw it.
- `making` at count one (V2): urea, creatinine and glucose forward, each with its rate in the species a source measures. Rejected: storage keeps V0's two columns.
- `using_n::<N>`, with the column dividing by `N`, on the used side only (V3): all four forward. Rejected for its extension to the made side: urea production, creatinine production, glucose output and glycogen synthesis are each stated per the made species, and V3 makes a law convert each to a used one.
- The count sealed in every column: all four of the slice's trace tests failed, since its model hash moved and each trace's seal carries it. Rejected for the tagged seal, which leaves every count-one body's bytes alone.

## Consequences

- Tickets 14 and 16 write each reaction forward, its rate in the species its source states, with `react_at` taking that rate as `Rate<Q>`:
  - Glycogen stored with its burned share, at the liver's beds for ticket 14 and the muscle's for ticket 16: `Proportions::<Glucose, 20>::using(Space::Cells).uses::<Oxygen, 6>(Space::Cells).makes::<Glycogen, 19>(Space::Cells).makes::<CarbonDioxide, 6>(Space::Cells).makes::<Water, 25>(Space::Cells).energy_into(Space::Thermal)`, its rate the glucose used per second. A source that states glycogen made writes `Proportions::<Glycogen, 19>::making` and `uses::<Glucose, 20>` instead.
  - Urea from the amino acids, for ticket 14: `Proportions::<Urea>::making(Space::Cells).uses::<AminoAcids, 2>(Space::Cells).uses::<Oxygen, 6>(Space::Cells).makes::<CarbonDioxide, 5>(Space::Cells).makes::<Water, 5>(Space::Cells).energy_into(Space::Thermal)`, its rate the urea made per second.
  - Creatinine from body protein, for ticket 16: `Proportions::<Creatinine>::making(Space::Cells).uses::<BodyProtein, 3>(Space::Cells).uses::<Oxygen, 6>(Space::Cells).makes::<CarbonDioxide, 5>(Space::Cells).makes::<Water, 4>(Space::Cells).energy_into(Space::Thermal)`, its rate the creatinine made per second.
  - Glucose from lactate, its energy paid by fuel, for ticket 14: `Proportions::<Glucose>::making(Space::Cells).uses::<Lactate, 3>(Space::Cells).uses::<Oxygen, 3>(Space::Cells).makes::<CarbonDioxide, 3>(Space::Cells).makes::<Water, 3>(Space::Cells).energy_into(Space::Thermal)`, its rate the glucose made per second. Its uses hold more energy than its makes, so it warms its bed.
- Each is declared with `FragmentBuilder::reaction::<C, Q, N>`. The counts above are the bench's: they balance the elements, and synthetic energy contents set the heat. Each ticket sources its own proportions, its burned share and its fuel, and keeps this form.
- A bench that holds storage or glucose from lactate alone is refused as `UnnamedConservedQuantity`: six pools under four conserved quantities leave one direction that none of them names. In the body the glucose burn beside it names that direction, and a bench declares one at a rate of zero.
- The kernel's value is the reference species used or made per second, and the column's rate is that value over the count, so the record's exchange for a reaction column counts turnovers.
- A validity range on a reaction's law judges the kernel's own value, the reference per second, before the division.
- When `N` is no power of two, the division rounds the column's rate once, and every term multiplies that one rate, so each conserved quantity closes as it did before.
